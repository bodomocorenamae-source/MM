01 / Welcome to the exhibition

Every medium tells a story.

Welcome to Echoes & Algorithms, an interactive digital portfolio exploring the different forms of media and their role in everyday life.

From printed pages to digital platforms, media shapes how we communicate, learn, understand events, and connect with others. This exhibition invites visitors to examine the strengths and limitations of each medium and become more responsible media users.

“The message matters. The medium shapes how the message travels.”

A guiding idea for our media literacy journey.

03 / Compare the formats

Choosing the right medium

Media forms have different capabilities. The best choice depends on the message, audience, purpose, and available resources.

04 / Media in action

One message. Many channels.

Imagine your school is announcing an environmental awareness campaign. The same accurate message can be adapted for different media.

🌱 School Environmental Awareness Campaign

Print

Distribute flyers with the campaign date, goals, and instructions.

Broadcast

Share a short announcement through the school sound system.

Digital

Publish the complete campaign details on the school website.

New Media

Post updates and reminders on the school's official social page.

Film & Video

Create a short promotional video showing ways to participate.

Outdoor

Display campaign posters on bulletin boards and public spaces.

Key insight: Adapt the format to the audience, but keep the facts consistent and accurate across every channel.

05 / Media evaluation

Pause. Check. Think.

Before believing or sharing information, use the CLEAR method as a simple checklist.

CCheck the creator

Who made or published the content?

LLook for evidence

Are claims supported by facts and reliable sources?

EExamine the date

Is the information current and in context?

AAssess the purpose

Does it inform, persuade, entertain, or sell?

RReview other sources

Can trusted sources independently confirm it?
